02

Research Interests

Machine learning automation of Molecular Beam Epitaxy (MBE) for topological quantum materials synthesis; nonlinear transport phenomena (nonlinear Hall effect, in-plane Hall effect, full-wave mixing); Kagome lattice compounds (FeSn, Fe₃Sn, Co₃Sn); topological antiferromagnets, Weyl semimetals and altermagnets.

03

Technical Expertise

Epitaxial Thin-Film Synthesis
Molecular Beam Epitaxy (MBE) of intermetallic Kagome lattice compounds (FeSn, Fe₃Sn, Co₃Sn).
Micro & Nano Fabrication
Multi-step optical lithography for Hall bar devices (6/12-terminal). FIB-based single crystal device fabrications, including TEM lamella preparation and antimonide prototyping.
Structural & Chemical Characterization
Thin-film XRD, XRR, phi-scans for phase identification, thickness, and epitaxial relationships.
Advanced Microscopy
STEM/TEM for atomic-scale analysis. In-situ RHEED for surface reconstruction. AFM for surface roughness.
Magnetotransport Measurements
Nonlinear & in-plane Hall effect, full-wave mixing using oscilloscope-based techniques at low and room temperature.
Data Analysis & Modelling
Python, MATLAB, LabVIEW for transport data acquisition and analysis. Quantum transport modelling.
Machine Learning (Basics)
Applying supervised and unsupervised learning for data-driven discovery, including regression, classification, and clustering techniques for quantum materials research.
